HOW IT WORKS

01

Built for the way Brazilians actually work in English

Most programs teach generic English. This training starts from the specific pressure points reported by Brazilian professionals: mental translation before speaking, fear of making mistakes in front of the team, and difficulty disagreeing with senior leadership in a second language.

  • Meetings: open, run and close a discussion in English
  • Negotiations: propose, push back and protect your position
  • Presentations: report results to international leadership
  • Interviews: tell your story and defend your value in English
02

The Lego Block Chain method

Communication is built in reusable blocks — context, objective, language blocks, simulation, response, adaptation. Instead of memorizing sentences, you learn to assemble your message under pressure and adapt it to any professional situation.

  • Simulations based on your real meetings and deadlines
  • Correction at the exact moment the error happens
  • Language blocks that transfer across situations
03

São Paulo and online, built around executive schedules

In-person sessions in São Paulo, or the same method online for professionals who travel. Sessions are short, high-density and prepared around real commitments already in your calendar.

  • Individual executive coaching
  • Corporate programs for teams and leaders
  • Preparation for specific meetings, keynotes and interviews
04

Frequently asked questions

Do I need advanced English to start?

No. The program starts from a functional base (intermediate or above) and develops the professional communication you need for real workplace situations.

Is it in English or Portuguese?

Training is delivered in English with simulations of real work situations; Portuguese support accelerates understanding when needed.

Can my company hire the program for a team?

Yes. Daby runs corporate programs for teams and leaders who operate in international environments.

Where does it happen?

In-person in São Paulo, Brazil, or online worldwide.
